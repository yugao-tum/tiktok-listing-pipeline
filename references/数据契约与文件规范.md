# Artifact contracts

## `产品任务.json`

```json
{
  "schema_version": "1.0",
  "product_id": "example-product",
  "product_url": "https://example.com/products/handle",
  "variant": {"id": "variant_target", "sku": "SKU-WITH-FOOTREST", "options": ["Dark Grey with Footrest"]},
  "excluded_variants": [{"id": "variant_excluded", "sku": "SKU-WITHOUT-FOOTREST"}],
  "excluded_assets": ["725.jpg"],
  "target_language": "en",
  "image_analysis_mode": "gpt_in_app_browser_chatgpt",
  "base_record_id": "rec_xxx"
}
```

An identity-incomplete research job may be queued without a variant. Variant ID and SKU must both be present and match the manifest and draft before final QA. `base_record_id` is optional for local preparation. Credentials never belong in this file.

## Asset counts

Inventory counts are optional diagnostics with an explicit scope, not listing completion gates. Do not infer exhaustive page coverage or require extra downloads just to reconcile them:

- `source_position_count`: discovered placements, not necessarily every image on the page.
- `canonical_url_count`: normalized distinct URLs.
- `strict_unique_count`: distinct downloaded bytes by SHA-256.
- `effective_visual_count`: human/visual distinct content after near-duplicate review.

Do not compare counts from different scopes. `effective_visual_count` stays null/absent when no visual count was established; never fill it from file counts. Record selected-image count separately. Unselected images may have `download_status: not_selected` or be absent from the manifest.

Each manifest item should include source/section position, raw and canonical URL, variant scope, filename, hash, dimensions, duplicate pointers, text-language judgment, download state, and translation state.

## GPT 图片审计记录

`图片审计/GPT图片审计记录.json` must contain:

- `analysis_source: "gpt_in_app_browser_chatgpt"`;
- one `chatgpt_conversation_url` for the product;
- prompt/version and audit timestamp;
- one hash-matched item for every original used in the final sequence, including originals behind adopted translations;
- per-image product/variant match, visual-duplicate decision, text presence/language, mixed-model status, publish decision, commercial role, and reason;
- one hash-matched review for each adopted translated output, covering fidelity, residual non-English text, product/color preservation, and number/dimension preservation;
- `final_sequence_review` with the reviewed sequence hash and `PASS` status.

Local metadata can provide context but cannot approve visual suitability. Missing coverage for selected images or their source originals, stale hashes and unresolved selected images block QA. Missing/unreviewed/corrupt unused inventory and failed unused translation attempts do not block the listing. The report identifies `audit_scope: listing_selected`; a PASS is not a full-page asset audit.

## Copy and order

`文案/文案底稿.json` must contain:

- exact product/variant identity;
- Chinese title and TikTok English title;
- English bullets;
- modular detail-page sections;
- claim boundaries and TBC fields;
- `image_plan.recommended_order` with consecutive ranks, one concrete `asset` path, a meaningful Chinese `subtitle`, and optional `source_asset` for translated outputs;
- `image_plan.do_not_use` with reasons.

Never use an `A OR B` path in a final order.

## Translation manifest

Each item records source and output paths, input/output SHA-256, source/target language, approved replacements, batch attempt history, model/prompt version, dimensions, and visual/OCR QA status. Source and output paths must differ.

Translation planning requires `selected_for_listing: true`, `publish_decision: include`, and confirmed translation need. Unselected, uncertain or excluded items are skipped. If an image is removed from the final sequence, its old generated attempts need no further repair or review.

## QA and commit

QA uses `PASS`, `PASS_WITH_NON_BLOCKING_NOTES`, or `FAIL`. Missing/stale GPT image-audit evidence, wrong identity, excluded leakage, missing files, untranslated publish assets, failed translated-image review, broken ranks, invalid images, or failed record comparison are blocking.

`质量检查/质量检查报告.json` is the copy/evidence review and binds `draft_sha256` to the actual draft. `质量检查/自动校验报告.json` is produced by the validator and binds `artifact_sha256` to job, manifest, draft, audit, optional translation manifest and current final manifest. QA and state must both carry that exact snapshot hash before preparing a commit.

Translation `source` and `output` resolve relative to `英文翻译图片/图片翻译清单.json`; `input_sha256` and `output_sha256` are required. Publish `asset` and translated `source_asset` resolve relative to the product directory. Every selected non-original file must be a registered reviewed translation of an approved source; excluded source images cannot re-enter through translations. Excluded non-English images do not require translation.

The exact prompt fields and accepted audit decisions are in [分阶段提示词.md](分阶段提示词.md). Legacy `shared_product`, `yes_german` and `no_german_or_non_english_detected` remain accepted, but unknown/uncertain selected images block QA. A `shared_product` conclusion must still originate in the product conversation.

Consumer detail rendering includes only confirmed size/package facts and explicit `consumer_notes`; legacy `notes` and `tbc_do_not_publish` remain internal. `--allow-without-qa` produces `preview_records` with `committable: false` in the sidecar and does not advance state. Writers must reject previews; they are intentionally not shaped as `update_records`.

The Feishu commit receipt records product ID, record ID, artifact hash, field names and lengths, command/API result, readback result, timestamp, and any ignored fields. Empty ignored fields plus readback equality is the success condition.

## 问题处理记录（只在实际出错时创建）

`质量检查/问题处理记录.json` 使用 `{"issues": [...]}`。每个问题至少记录：`id`、`phase`、`symptom`、`required_for_goal`（明确用户要求及必要交付默认 true）、`affected_assets`（产品相对路径）、`cause`（未确认注明 hypothesis）、`attempts`（每次实际改变、结果和证据）、`status`。禁止把必需修复在失败后自行改成可选。

| status | 关闭条件 |
|---|---|
| resolved | `resolution_summary` 和 `verification_evidence`：原失败步骤已按正确标准复测通过，不只“文件存在” |
| replaced | `replacement_asset` 必须进入当前序列且有 `verification_evidence`，能满足原必需角色；不表示旧文件修好 |
| excluded_optional | 仅 `required_for_goal: false`、不在当前序列／其来源中，且有 `reason`、`coverage_evidence`；表示可选排除，不是修复 |
| deferred_optional | 同上，并有 `next_action`；用于已验证恢复路径后的非阻塞残留问题，不宣称根治 |
| open / diagnosing / repairing / blocked | 尚未闭环；自动 QA 不得把已登记的未解决项算作完成 |

问题记录存在时纳入 QA 快照及提交前哈希；删除或修改后必须重跑检查。自动校验只确认字段和状态一致性，证据是否足以满足用户目标仍须由执行者核实。没有实际问题就不用增加空表或额外审计。

`detail_page` 可包含自定义消费者模块（如 adjustment、fit、controls），按底稿顺序渲染 heading/body/confirmed，不限固定键名；`notes`、`internal*`、`tbc*`、`claim_evidence` 及 `publish: false` 模块不输出给消费者。准备载荷时核对源模块覆盖，回读一致不能证明渲染前没有漏段。
